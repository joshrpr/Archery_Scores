Installing

  1. Tap Download for Android above, then open the file when it finishes.
  2. Android asks whether to allow installs from your browser. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect says it doesn't recognise the developer, tap More details then Install anyway. The app isn't on the Play Store yet, so Google hasn't seen it before.

The app tells you when there's a new version. Tap Get it and install over the top; your scores stay put.

What it does

  • One phone scores the whole group, IBO 3D or Vegas 300
  • Plot each arrow on the target with a magnifier and haptic ticks
  • Shot maps, group size and sight drift over time
  • Personal bests, trends and head-to-head analytics
  • Everything stays on your phone; back up to Google Drive any time
