# Ropers Archery Scorecard

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

The offline web app also ships in an Android Capacitor wrapper. Its current interface uses the same web design on both surfaces.

## Users

Archers recording individual and group rounds. The current phone used for validation is a Google Pixel 10 Pro running Android 17.

## Product Purpose

Record IBO 3D and Vegas 300 scores, review scorecards and history, and understand performance without depending on a connection.

## Operating Context

Score entry during a round, one target or end at a time. Vegas rounds contain ten ends of three arrows. Users can enter score values or plot arrow positions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Group archers, round history, analytics, backups, and exports already exist.
- Vegas X counts ten and breaks ties; a miss stays zero. Target colors are gold, red, and blue regardless of interface palette.
- Completion must save before presenting celebration and keep the scorecard accessible.
- Preserve offline operation, readable controls, enlarged text, reduced motion, and Android Back behavior.
- The approved completion animation uses a rapid recap followed by one emphasized final arrow.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Ropers Archery Scorecard.
- The user likes the existing orange and requests three mockups centered on it.
- Blue is optional in the interface. The user wants a more intentional, less template-like overall aesthetic.
- The user selected Velocity OD for the app and Ranch Horizon for the icon/logo and authorized implementation of the selected prototype direction.
- The ranch aerial in the existing logo must remain part of the icon/logo identity; simplification must not remove it. Use the existing image rather than generating a replacement landscape.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing application source in `ibo-scorer/`; approved animation studies in `assets/prototypes/vegas-arrow/`. Any names, dates, rounds, or scores introduced for visual comparisons must be explicitly identified as sample content.

## Product Principles

- Score accuracy and reliable access come first.
- Keep frequent score entry clear and quick.
- Preserve real target coloring separately from interface branding.
- Compare proposals before adopting a new visual system.
